# Getting a pilot or LOI

The Wave 1 judge ranked by **feasibility, real-world adoption and uniqueness**. A single real conversation with a bank or fintech is worth more than any feature.

## Who to approach (fastest first)

1. **Co-operative and small-finance banks.** They're hit hard by mule accounts, have smaller fraud teams, and decide faster.
2. **Payment aggregators and fintech apps** (PA/PG licence holders). They carry the payout risk and want pre-payment checks.
3. **Fraud and risk leads at larger banks**, on LinkedIn (titles: "Head of Fraud Risk", "FRM", "Transaction Monitoring").
4. **Industry bodies and sandboxes:** a regulator's sandbox cohort, fintech associations.

## Message template

> Subject: Sharing mule-account signals across banks without sharing customer data
>
> Hi [name],
>
> We're building MuleNet: banks flag suspected mule accounts together, and an account is flagged only when several independent banks report it. The account number and the reporting bank's identity are never revealed. It uses zero-knowledge proofs on Midnight, and payee checks happen locally with no data leaving your systems.
>
> Could we have 20 minutes of your time to learn how your team handles mule accounts today? If it fits, we'd value a short non-binding letter of intent to pilot.
>
> [name, link to demo video, link to repo]

## What counts as evidence for judges

- A named contact who's willing to be quoted, or a non-binding LOI (one page).
- Notes from 3–5 discovery calls (problems heard, current tools, willingness to pay).

Put these in the deck **only if they're real**.
